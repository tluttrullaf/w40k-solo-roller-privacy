Privacy Policy

This app does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

No user accounts are required. The app does not use analytics, advertising services, or tracking technologies. No personal or device information is transmitted off the device.

All features operate fully offline. Any data entered into the app is stored locally on the user’s device only and can be removed by uninstalling the app.

If you have questions about this policy, you may contact the developer via the email address listed on the Google Play Store page.
